Privacy Policy

FOS ERP Mobile (Native CRM app) · Last updated 17 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FOS ERP Mobile (the "App"), operated by Kology ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App on Android or iOS. The App is a companion mobile CRM for FOS ERP customers with an active tenant account — it is used to manage leads, companies, follow-ups, quotations, and sales orders on the go. It does not include warehouse, purchasing, or admin functions; those remain on the desktop/web application.

1. Information we collect

Account & tenant information

To sign in, you provide a company tenant identifier, your username/email, and password. This is used solely to authenticate you against your organization's FOS ERP account and is not shared with any other tenant or third party.

CRM business data

Data typePurpose
Contact details (name, email, phone) of leads/companies/contactsCore CRM functionality — managing your organization's sales pipeline
Photos / imagesBusiness card capture and CRM attachments you choose to add to a record
Voice recordingsOptional voice notes attached to follow-ups or meetings, at your choice
Quotation / sales order detailsReviewing and progressing deals from the field

All CRM business data belongs to your employer/organization (the "tenant") and is stored on that tenant's FOS ERP account. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

2. Device permissions

PermissionWhy we ask
CameraScan warehouse barcodes/QR codes, capture business cards, and attach CRM photos/documents
Photo libraryImport an existing photo for business cards or CRM attachments
MicrophoneRecord optional CRM voice notes on follow-ups and meetings

We do not request access to your device location, contacts list, SMS, or call log, and the App does not run any background data collection.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in to your organization's tenant
  • To display, create, and update CRM records (leads, companies, follow-ups, quotations, sales orders) on your behalf
  • To attach photos/voice notes you explicitly capture to the relevant CRM record
  • To diagnose crashes and technical issues so we can keep the App working reliably

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

4. Data sharing

Data you enter is visible only within your own organization's tenant, according to the roles and permissions your administrator has configured. We do not share your data with other tenants or external advertising networks. Data may be processed by our infrastructure/hosting providers strictly to operate the service (e.g., cloud hosting, database, email delivery), under confidentiality obligations.

5. Data security

All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to tenant data requires authentication, and each request is scoped to your tenant so that one organization cannot see another's data.

6. Data retention & deletion

CRM data is retained for as long as your organization maintains an active FOS ERP tenant. To request deletion of your account or personal data, contact your organization's FOS ERP administrator, or reach us directly using the contact details below.

7. Children's privacy

The App is a business tool intended for use by employees of organizations using FOS ERP. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at privacy@kology.co.

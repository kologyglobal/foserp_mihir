# Kology Accounting Flow

## Money In

Sales Invoice → Receipt → Allocation → Outstanding / Ageing / Credit Notes

## Money Out / Expenses

A. Paid expense → journal/payment → Bank/Cash ↓ → Expense GL  
B. Vendor Bill → AP → Vendor Payment → Allocation

## General

Journals, GL, Trial Balance; P&L/BS via posted GL (live FS polish deferred).

## Bank & Cash

Accounts, statements, reconciliation, transfers — reuse treasury module.
